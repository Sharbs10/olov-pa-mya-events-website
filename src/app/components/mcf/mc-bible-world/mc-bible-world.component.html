MC Bible World

A shared Minecraft world for building, exploring, and learning together.

What this is

MCF Bible World is a Minecraft Bedrock Realm for the MCF grades 5th-8th.

The students will be able to creatively build their favorite Bible stories.

  1. Parents will be emailed a password to unlock the Realm link.

Expectations

  • Be kind and respectful to everyone in the Realm.
  • Respect other players' builds and belongings; No Griefing other students' builds.
  • Follow any guidance from the Realm host and MCF leaders.
  • Feel free to work on your builds throughout the week. We will have a show and tell at the end of class to share your progress.

Specs to be able to join

  • A device that supports Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
  • Minecraft Bedrock Edition installed and updated.
  • An internet connection and a Microsoft account to play online.
